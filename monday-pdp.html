Why watsonx.data

Purpose-built for AI workloads, watsonx.data enables you to scale analytics and AI with a data store built on an open data lakehouse architecture.

Cost optimization

Reduce your data warehouse costs by up to 50% through workload optimization across multiple query engines.

Open format

Built on open formats including Apache Iceberg, Apache Parquet, and Apache Orc for data portability.

Governed access

Centralized governance with fine-grained access control, data lineage, and audit trails built in.

Hyperscaler availability

watsonx.data is available on AWS and Microsoft Azure in addition to IBM Cloud. Select your deployment target during configuration.
